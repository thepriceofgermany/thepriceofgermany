Grand Classic Ballet 2026/27: Swan Lake and The Nutcracker in 50 German Cities

I grew up loving the ballet, and this year I'm taking my daughter to her first one. Here is where and when you can catch Grand Classic Ballet across Germany this winter.

Going to the Nutcracker on Christmas Eve was a core memory of mine as a kid, and this year I can't wait to share it with my daughter. Grand Classic Ballet is touring Germany this winter with two timeless classics, and there is probably a show near you. If you are looking for Grand Classic Ballet tickets, here is everything you need: what is playing, which cities, what it costs, and how to book.

I'm Justin, an American who has spent more than a decade living in Germany and writing about what life here actually costs. A night at the ballet is one of the rare things that is genuinely cheaper and easier to get to here than back home, and this tour is a good excuse to go.

What is playing

Swan Lake (Schwanensee). Tchaikovsky's most famous ballet, the story of Odette and the swan curse, danced to one of the most recognizable scores ever written. This season marks exactly 150 years since the music was first heard, which makes it a fitting year to see it live.

The Nutcracker (Der Nussknacker). The holiday classic, and the one I grew up watching around Christmas. Clara, the toy soldier, the Sugar Plum Fairy, and a score you already know by heart even if you have never been to a ballet. It is the most family-friendly show on the tour and the easiest first ballet for a child.

There are also two special formats on the tour: a Christmas show called "Im Zauber der Weihnacht" and a behind-the-scenes format called "Jenseits der Bühne" that shows how the company rehearses and stages the productions.

150 shows in 50 cities

The winter tour runs from November 14, 2026 in Bonn to February 17, 2027 in Neustadt, with 150 performances across 50 cities. Wherever you are in Germany, there is a good chance a show is coming within a short train ride. Some of the bigger stops:

  • Berlin
  • Munich
  • Hamburg
  • Cologne
  • Frankfurt
  • Stuttgart
  • Nuremberg
  • Düsseldorf
  • Bonn
  • Hannover
  • Erfurt
  • Bayreuth

In Berlin the shows run from December 21, 2026 to January 27, 2027 at the Uber Eats Music Hall. Berlin State Ballet stars Iana Salenko and Marian Walter dance as guests in selected performances, including the Berlin premiere on December 22. In Stuttgart there is a New Year's Eve Swan Lake at the Apollo Theater and a January date at the Beethoven-Saal.

Dates and venues change city by city, so the fastest way to find your show is to open the ticket page, type your city, and see what comes up.

What do tickets cost?

Ticket prices depend on the city, the hall, and how close to the stage you want to sit. As a rough guide for the 2026/27 tour:

Typical ticket prices (2026/27)
Entry seatsfrom ~30 EUR
Mid-tier seats~45 to 65 EUR

For comparison, a decent seat at a major Nutcracker production in a big US city routinely runs 80 to 150 dollars or more. Here you can take the whole family to a full orchestra ballet for the price of one premium American ticket, which is a big part of why we go every year.

How to book your tickets

  1. Open the ticket page and search for your city or the nearest big one.

  2. Pick the show you want, Swan Lake or The Nutcracker, and a date that works. Weekend afternoons are the easiest with kids.

  3. Choose your seats from the hall map. Middle of the first balcony is usually the best value for seeing the whole stage.

  4. Book early for Christmas week. Nutcracker shows around December 23 to 27 sell out first, so the sooner you book, the better the seats.

Frequently asked questions

How much do Grand Classic Ballet tickets cost in Germany?
Prices start at around 30 euros for the cheaper seats and rise to roughly 100 euros for premium seats in the biggest venues. The exact price depends on the city, the hall and the seat, so the ticket page shows the live price for each show.
Which German cities is Grand Classic Ballet touring in 2026/27?
The winter tour reaches 50 cities with 150 performances, including Berlin, Munich, Hamburg, Cologne, Frankfurt, Stuttgart, Nuremberg, Düsseldorf, Bonn and Hannover. Dates and venues change city by city, so search the ticket page for the show nearest you.
When does the Grand Classic Ballet winter tour run?
The 2026/27 tour runs from November 14, 2026 in Bonn to February 17, 2027 in Neustadt. In Berlin the shows run from December 21, 2026 to January 27, 2027 at the Uber Eats Music Hall.
What ballets are being performed?
The two main productions are Swan Lake (Schwanensee) and The Nutcracker (Der Nussknacker), both to Tchaikovsky's music. The tour also includes a Christmas format called "Im Zauber der Weihnacht" and a behind-the-scenes format called "Jenseits der Bühne."
Is The Nutcracker a good first ballet for children?
Yes. The Nutcracker is one of the most family-friendly ballets, with a Christmas story, a famous score and about two hours of runtime including an interval. It is a popular choice for a child's first ballet in Germany over the holidays.
